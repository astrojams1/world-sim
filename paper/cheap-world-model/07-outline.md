## Abstract
Problem (metric scene state from images for robotics, without training or calibration); system (cheap VLM
+ sandbox + classical pipeline, one call); headline (99.5 mean, 6/10 exact, 22 s, $0.032, 6.3k tokens;
95.9 at 8 objects) [A.record, A.capacity]; the finding (accuracy came from tool-ification: 90.0/232 s → 99.5/22 s
across 26 iterations; pipeline alone matches) [A.baseline, A.history, OFF]; scope word (synthetic benchmark).

## 8 Limitations
Synthetic; two colors, two shapes, three sizes; ≤ 12 objects; tuned on 10 rooms with an offline-only
held-out guard (API held-out never run); one model/provider; cost floor is the sandbox; "training-free"
means no training by us — the VLM is trained; no dynamics.
